A to-do list that won't be ignored

Pop it before it fills your screen.

TODOPOP turns every task into a bubble that floats over whatever you're doing and slowly grows. Finish the task, pop the bubble. Leave it, and it takes over.

How it works

Three steps. One of them is satisfying.

  1. Blow a bubble

    Give it a name and choose how urgent it is. Add a link or a deadline if the task has one.

  2. Watch it grow

    Urgent bubbles grow faster. In the last day before a deadline, a bubble moves up a level all by itself.

  3. Pop it

    Done? Pop it and watch it burst. Popped keeps a record of everything you finished this week and this month.

Made for focus

Gentle when you're working. Hard to ignore when you're not.

Get in the bubble

Click a bubble to step inside. The edges of your screen take on your color, a timer counts your focus, and you can break the task into smaller steps that float around you.

Over everything

Bubbles float above your other apps and let your clicks pass straight through to whatever is underneath. The control panel is one shortcut away: Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + Space.

Groups in color

Sort bubbles into up to six groups, each with its own soft color. Send a group to one edge of the screen so school stays on the left and work on the right.

Pause growing

Need a break? Pause for 30 minutes, an hour, until tomorrow morning, or until you say so. Paused bubbles wear a dashed outline.

Phone app

Your phone is the remote.

Add a task on the bus and it's floating on your computer before you get home. Pop it from your phone or your computer: they always show the same list. No email, no password.

  1. On your computer, open TODOPOP, click the third O, then Get the phone app.
  2. Scan the code with your phone's camera.
  3. Keep it on your home screen: Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or ⋮ → Install app on Android. Open it from there and tap Scan code once more.

Opening it the first time

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
  1. That's because TODOPOP is new and isn't signed by Microsoft yet. Nothing is wrong with your download.
  2. Click More info, then Run anyway. TODOPOP installs and opens.
  3. You'll only see this once.
Mac says TODOPOP "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it
  1. Open the downloaded TODOPOP-mac.dmg and drag TODOPOP into Applications.
  2. Open TODOPOP from Applications. If macOS blocks it, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to TODOPOP. Confirm with your password.
  4. TODOPOP lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen, not the Dock. You'll only need to do this once.
